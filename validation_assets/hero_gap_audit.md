# Hero Gap Audit

## Snapshot
- Blueprint: [design/workspace-prototype/index.html](../design/workspace-prototype/index.html)
- Current: [app/templates/components/hero.html](../app/templates/components/hero.html)
- Side-by-side reference: [validation_assets/blueprint_vs_converigo_side_by_side.png](blueprint_vs_converigo_side_by_side.png)

================================================

Gap #1

Lokasi
Hero decorations / background layer

Blueprint
Eight small floating file-style icons distributed around the hero edges, creating layered depth and a light premium backdrop.

Current
Two large blurred decorative pills and a much simpler background treatment.

Perbedaan
The current hero loses the blueprint’s icon-based detail and feels less premium and less layered.

Target
Restore the hero decoration layer to the blueprint’s visual language while keeping the upload card unchanged.

Status
NOT MATCH

================================================

Gap #2

Lokasi
Hero headline block

Blueprint
A large, centered hero title with strong blue gradient emphasis, compact line-height, and a clear premium hierarchy.

Current
The title is centered, but its scale, weight, and rhythm feel slightly less refined and less aligned with the blueprint.

Perbedaan
The heading hierarchy is visually weaker and less polished than the blueprint.

Target
Adjust the title scale, line-height, and gradient emphasis to better match the blueprint.

Status
NOT MATCH

================================================

Gap #3

Lokasi
Hero shell spacing

Blueprint
Balanced vertical spacing between the title, supporting text, and card stage.

Current
The hero feels slightly more stretched vertically and the headline-to-card spacing is less balanced.

Perbedaan
The layout rhythm is less compact and less elegant than the blueprint.

Target
Tighten the hero padding and reduce the headline-to-stage gap to match the blueprint more closely.

Status
NOT MATCH

================================================

Gap #4

Lokasi
Card stage wrapper

Blueprint
The card sits in a lighter, cleaner frame with subtle border and shadow treatment.

Current
The stage wrapper feels more visually heavy and dominant than the blueprint.

Perbedaan
The hero card stage is framed more aggressively than intended.

Target
Make the card stage wrapper lighter, calmer, and more restrained.

Status
NOT MATCH

================================================

Gap #5

Lokasi
Supporting paragraph

Blueprint
The supporting copy is concise, centered, and placed with a slightly more elegant vertical rhythm.

Current
The paragraph feels slightly denser and less refined in rhythm.

Perbedaan
The copy spacing and visual cadence do not yet feel as polished as the blueprint.

Target
Refine paragraph spacing and line-height to better match the blueprint.

Status
NOT MATCH
