# AI Overview

## Purpose of AI_BRAIN
AI_BRAIN serves as Converigo's internal AI foundation for organizing trusted project intelligence, preserving operational context, and aligning AI behavior across teams and tools. Its primary responsibility is governance of AI-facing knowledge domains so outputs remain consistent, reliable, and aligned with engineering standards.

## Knowledge
The knowledge domain is responsible for maintaining structured, authoritative project context. It defines what the AI ecosystem should know about system boundaries, architecture, conventions, workflows, and decision history.

## Memory
The memory domain is responsible for persistent contextual state. It captures durable organizational understanding, including recurring patterns, institutional decisions, issue histories, and other long-lived references needed for continuity across sessions and contributors.

## Generated
The generated domain is responsible for housing machine-produced intelligence artifacts derived from approved sources. It acts as the managed output space for AI-consumable metadata and structured derivatives that support discovery and grounding.

## Scripts
The scripts domain is responsible for operational lifecycle support of AI_BRAIN assets. It defines controlled, repeatable processes for maintaining AI data quality, consistency, and readiness across the platform.

## RAG
The RAG domain is responsible for retrieval governance. It defines how relevant context is selected, prioritized, and delivered to AI interactions so responses are grounded in trusted internal knowledge.

## Configuration
The configuration domain is responsible for policy and behavioral controls. It establishes declarative standards for what is included, excluded, recognized, and emitted across AI_BRAIN processes to ensure predictable platform behavior.
